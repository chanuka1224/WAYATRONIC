    About WayaTronic

    Precision Tools. Smarter Electronics. Empowering Sri Lanka's Engineers & Builders.

    Our Story

    Founded to solve the persistent shortage of genuine, calibrated electronic test equipment in Sri Lanka, WayaTronic serves as the reliable bridge between global instrument manufacturers and local engineering talent.

    From university electronic laboratories and robotic researchers to industrial power systems engineers and passionate makers, we provide the test gear you need to measure with absolute precision.

    Uncompromising Calibration & Authenticity

    Test equipment is only as good as its precision. Every digital multimeter, oscilloscope, and bench power supply imported by WayaTronic is certified against standard NIST references. We test voltage dividers, frequency counters, and thermal characteristics before certifying inventory for dispatch.

    Island-Wide Coverage

    Whether your lab is situated at the University of Moratuwa, University of Peradeniya, or an industrial automation facility in Hambantota or Jaffna, our specialized logistics network delivers safely in protective flight cases with full warranty backup.
